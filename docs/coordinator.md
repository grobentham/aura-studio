# Unified Producer guide
Scan up to 14.9 seconds from playhead, 1–8 audible tracks, 48 kHz before master dynamics and at unity master gain. Sequential isolated channel renders feed existing Producer, Mix Next and Analyse algorithms in a worker. Full mix gets loudness/peak/clipping, tentative key and BPM, and stereo diagnostic. Review conservative fader attenuation (skip automated volume) and broad EQ cuts against an explicitly selected priority track. Default no selections. Render original and proposed mix from cloned track data; block increases in clipped samples or excessive peak, reject inaudible renders. RMS-matched audition with peak cap. User applies selected validated changes as one undo. Snapshot and selected-change guards prevent stale application. All other stages are explicit links to specialised tools, not unattended automatic execution. No paid AI or audio upload. Excerpt metrics do not ensure full-song safety, clipping repair, professional mixing or artistic quality. 
Also fixes shared bus/master disconnection on deletion of a routed track: groupDestination is a borrowed node, not owned by the track. Regression checks shared destination is not disconnected.
