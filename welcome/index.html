Everything you need for a first song

You start with a full song and make it yours, instead of an empty screen.

🎤

Hum a song

Sing or hum a melody into your microphone. Aura finds the notes, the key and the tempo, then arranges a full track in the style you pick.

♫

Remix famous melodies

Turn Ode to Joy, Canon in D, Für Elise and more into lo-fi, trap, house, cinematic or reggaeton tracks. Every Songbook melody is in the public domain.

🎻

Real instruments

Piano, strings, guitars, horns, marimba and more come from real recordings with several dynamics per note, not cheap beeps.

●

Record your voice

Put on headphones, press record and sing over your song. Your vocal lands on its own track, ready to mix.

⇣

Export and share

Download a finished WAV, separate stems for another studio, or MIDI notes.

🔒

Private by default

Your songs and recordings stay in your browser. You can back them up to a folder you choose.

Questions

Is Aura free?

Yes. Everything is free while Aura is in beta, including every remix style, Hum a song and remixing your own melodies.

Do I need an account or an app?

No. Aura runs in a current web browser on a computer, tablet or phone (Chrome and Edge work best). You can add it to your home screen if you like.

Where are my songs saved?

In your browser, on your device. Open Projects → Backups & sync to back them up to a folder (for example Dropbox or OneDrive) or download a copy. Clearing your browser data deletes songs you haven't backed up.

Can I use the songs I make?

Your melodies, lyrics and recordings are yours. The Songbook melodies are public domain. The built-in sounds are free-licensed: most are CC0 and a few are CC BY 3.0, so see the sound credits for attribution details.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Phones get a layout with large buttons and a tab bar at the bottom. For editing notes, turning your phone sideways gives you more room.
