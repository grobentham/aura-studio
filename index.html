Make room for your music.

01 / Make music

Choose Instruments, then a keyboard, guitar, synth, or drum kit. Tap to play, or use A–K on your computer. R records notes; Z / X changes octave. Choose a one- or two-bar count-in under the transport, or turn it off. Connect MIDI to play a hardware controller in a supported browser.

02 / Record audio

Choose Record, select your input, and Connect input. Allow the browser microphone prompt, then Record audio. Finish take or Stop adds the recording to a new track. Use headphones for monitoring.

03 / Arrange & mix

Drag clips to arrange. Drag the right edge to resize; Alt-drag or Duplicate copies. Select an audio clip for Trim, Split at playhead, and Normalize. The mixer has volume, pan, tone, reverb, echo, mute, and solo. Select a channel for three-band EQ and compression. Audio clips have fades. The master has compression and limiting.

04 / Build with loops

Preview a loop, then Add at the current bar. Patterns follow your project tempo and key. Click the ruler to move the playhead. Drag across it to set a cycle.

05 / Keep your session

Save creates a recovery checkpoint on this device. Projects holds your sessions and the 12 latest saved versions. Download project makes a portable JSON backup with embedded audio; Import project restores it. Keep a backup before clearing browser data. Audio stays on this device.

06 / Share your sound

Export song renders a 16- or 24-bit WAV offline, at 44.1 or 48 kHz. Export the full mix, aligned stems in a ZIP, or instrument notes as MIDI. Choose the entire song or your cycle range. Keep the tab open until rendering finishes.
